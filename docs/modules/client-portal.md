# Client Portal

> The client dashboard: a private, sign-in-free, read-mostly page per project (`/portal/<token>`) that the agency sends to a client. It answers three questions: where the project is (status, the current stage with a live progress bar, the team's one-line note, that stage's files), what the client gets and when (every stage of the **client plan** with its dates, "what you get" lines and files), and what we need from them (open asks). Project-wide files, and one Approve button when a checklist stage carries the `client_review` role (the only write a client can make), sit below. The plan (`projects/{id}.clientPlan`) is drafted from the project's own checklist when the project is created (New project dialog, owned by [project-links.md](./project-links.md)) or from the Client tab, and its progress is read back off that checklist, so ticking steps in the Delivery tab moves the client's tracker; the team can pin the stage by hand. The team runs it all from the **Client** tab (link, Now, Plan) and sees client-facing signals on the project dashboard (status chip, "shared / waiting on client / stale" counts, a "Waiting on client" filter, a "Client updates due" banner). The token in the URL is the only credential. Everything the client receives is built server-side by one allow-list projection, `buildClientPortalView`.

**Since 2026-09-28 the Timeline tab drives the stages whenever it has milestones to show** (Rehan's call: six live projects had a timeline, none a saved plan). Each Timeline phase is a stage, its milestones the steps inside it (title, state, dates), and the current stage is the earliest phase with a milestone not done; the team can hide a milestone and attach files per phase (`projects/{id}.clientTimeline`). Projects without a timeline keep the checklist plan. **Meetings** come from Fathom: an hourly cron files each call with someone from the client's email domain under the project (`projects/{id}/meetings`) and under the stage its date falls in, as `pending`. The client sees a call (names, Fathom's or the team's summary, next steps, the Fathom share link) only after someone opens it in the Client tab and presses Share. On the page, each stage carries its own milestones, meetings and files: the current one under "Where we are", the others behind a disclosure in "The plan, stage by stage".

**Since 2026-10-01 a project's Google Sheet can drive the page** (Rehan's calls: the team writes the sheet, the app only reads it; plan in [project-sheet-contract.md](../plans/project-sheet-contract.md)). The team binds a sheet on the Client tab; the app reads it into a server-only snapshot (`project_sheets/{projectId}`) on bind, on "Sync now" and every 15 minutes, and the portal renders from that snapshot, never from Google. Tabs are recognised by name only: five core kinds (Overview, Timeline, Tracker, Client Inputs, Change Log), three modules (Launch Checklist, SEO Tags, Redirects), any `[Fill this]` tab as a client ask; everything else is ignored unless the team maps it. When bound, the sheet's Timeline drives the stages (unless the team picks the app's), and the page gains a header fact line, "Your turn", "The work" (trackers with filters), "Launch readiness" and "Changes outside the agreed scope"; sheet inputs merge with task asks. Notes, assignees, contacts and money columns are never read.

**Also since 2026-10-01 the Checklist is where the team marks progress, and the client's process follows it** (Rehan's calls: the team ticks the Checklist; projects are made as one of four engagements, Development only, Web Design only, Web Design + Development, Brand + Web + Development, with Copy as an add-on; Web Design and Copy SOPs drafted for his review). Each SOP in the Checklist Creator is tagged with the service it delivers and says what the client sees of it: a section is one client step (`clientStep`, e.g. "Moodboarding"), an item inside it can be a step of its own (e.g. "Feedback on moodboard", `clientWho: 'client'`) or be hidden, and everything unlabelled stays internal. A step is done when all its items are, dated by a date the team recorded on an item ("Held on") or else the day of the last tick; a client's step that is In progress reads "Waiting on you". Where stages come from is decided in one place, `portalStageSources`: the sheet's Process, with each sheet step that shares a name with a checklist step moved along by it (further along wins, so neither source pulls the other back); else a checklist whose SOP labels it, on the checklist itself or borrowed from its SOP by section and item title (`planSource: 'checklist'`, shown as the numbered process); else the Timeline tab; else the plan. **Later the same day the Client tab went read-only** (Rehan: "Shouldn't this be managed by the Checklist instead of us doing things manually… make this page very lean"): no Now card, no plan editor, no Timeline editor, no pinned stages (an old `currentStageId` is ignored; Delivered still shows everything done). The current stage is the furthest one where *our* work has started (a client step under way, like assets sent early, does not count), so an unticked housekeeping item in Kickoff no longer holds a client there. With several checklists on a project, stages run in working order (Kickoff, Brand Design, Copy, Web Design, Development, Launch, Handover), not in the order the checklists were made. Every checklist tick stamps `clientFacing.lastUpdateAt`, which is what keeps a portal off "Client updates due" now that nobody marks it updated by hand.

**Later on 2026-10-01 the app began making the sheet itself and keeping it from the checklist** (Rehan: the sheet should be "the starting point", one per project, "in sync continuously with any update in Checklist"; his calls: checklist → sheet one way, shaped per engagement, created in a Shared Drive). The Client tab's (and the Delivery tab's Pages stage's) "Project sheet" card is now where a sheet starts: one-time setup (a Shared Drive with the service account as Content manager, link pasted once, stored in `app_settings/project_sheets`), then "Create the project sheet". The app creates `<Client> · Project Sheet` in that Drive with six tabs (Overview, Process, Pages, What we need, and since the same evening Checklist and SEO), only the stages the project's checklists have, and rewrites it from the checklist's client steps, the page tracker and the `needsClientInput` tasks: about 10 s after any tick, page change or new ask (`requestProjectSheetWrite`, debounced per project) and every 15 minutes from the cron (Timeline edits only reach it through the cron). Its Process tab is whatever the client's page shows, from the same `portalStageSources`. A write is skipped when nothing changed (content hash). Such a sheet is `managed: true` on its `project_sheets` record and is **never read back**: the portal's stages come from the checklist as before, and edits made in the sheet are replaced (every tab carries a warning-only protection saying so). A project still on a hand-kept sheet (Different AI) keeps reading it and is offered "Replace with a sheet the app keeps", which binds the new one and leaves the old file alone.


**Deliverables tabs (2026-10-01).** Rehan: the sheet should read like the Dreamteam Website Plan, with SEO and AEO on one tab. A checklist section with a `sheetTab` (Checklist Creator: "On the sheet", with a one-line `summary`) is a band of that tab: `sheetBoards` builds one `SheetBoard` per tab (sections borrowed from the SOP by title for older checklists; hidden items left out; owner, priority and week from the item or its SOP item; the client's name as owner for client deliverables), and `sheetPlan` the Overview's PLAN roll-up (deliverables, P0, done, progress; Not needed counts for nothing). The writer's `writeBoards` adds missing tabs after "What we need" and redraws each in full on every write: a title band and summary band (#24124D), a purple header row (# · Deliverable · Owner · Priority · Status · Week · Notes), rows fitted to their text, P0/P1/P2 and status chips as direct formats, weeks written as text. `LAYOUT_VERSION` 4 renamed "SEO" to "Page SEO" and replaced the old flat Checklist tab. The SEO & AEO and Analytics & Tracking SOPs come from `scripts/seed-seo-aeo-sops.ts`.

**Metrics Tracker (2026-10-01).** `projects/{id}/metrics/{key}` (server-only: no rule, so client SDKs are denied; written by `scripts/metrics.ts` with firebase-admin): `ProjectMetric` in [project-metrics.ts](../../src/modules/client-portal/domain/project-metrics.ts) (`name`, `source`, `order`, `baselineMonth`, `values` by "YYYY-MM", `sources` by month, `lowerIsBetter`, `unit`). `DEFAULT_METRICS` (13: GA4 sessions, users, organic, conversions, conversion rate; Search Console indexed pages, impressions, clicks, CTR; PageSpeed mobile LCP and CLS; CrUX INP; AI citations of 10 prompts). `sheetMetrics` gives the baseline month and five after it, and the change to the latest value (% for counts, points for rates, better/worse by `lowerIsBetter`). The writer's `writeMetrics` draws the "Metrics Tracker" tab (before Page SEO) in the band style; labels and changes are written with a leading apostrophe so Sheets keeps them text. `npm run metrics -- show|init|set`; a write rewrites the project sheet.

**Calls on the process (2026-10-01).** `PortalProcess` shows each shared Fathom call with a round video mark, its date and length, and "Watch recording": under the step done that day (the kickoff call under "Kickoff call"), else as a row of its own in the stage; the Links sidebar lists the recordings too.

**Links on steps (2026-10-01).** Rehan: the client should see the moodboard, the sitemap and other contextual links. A checklist `url` field marked `forClient` (the SOP's Sitemap, Moodboard, Wireframes, Homepage design, Inner pages design, Figma file, Staging site; the agency basics' Project sheet, Walkthrough videos and Handover doc) puts the link recorded there on the step its item belongs to: `checklistProcess` sets `url` and `urlLabel` (the field's label; the latest recorded wins), the portal's process shows a button named after it, and the sheet's Process tab shows it in Link. Older checklists borrow the mark from their SOP by item title; the fields were added to every existing checklist the same day. On wide screens the portal shows every one of these links in a sticky **Links** sidebar beside the page (`portalLinks` in [portal-links.ts](../../src/modules/client-portal/domain/portal-links.ts): the website, each step's link in process order with the step as its note, then the shared files, deduped by URL; `PortalLinksSidebar`), and "Files & links" at the bottom shows only below `lg`. Project links still reach the portal only when opted in; the sheet's Key links leave out ClickUp, Slack and Fathom team links (`clientKeyLinks`).

## Key flows

1. **Enable and share a link**
   1. Team clicks *Enable portal* in `PortalLinkCard` → `clientPortalRepository.setLink(id, 'enable')` → `POST /api/client-portal/[id]/link`.
   2. Route: `requireProjectAccess`; if `clientPortal.enabled && getActivePortalToken()` → return existing state; else `issuePortalToken`.
   3. `issuePortalToken`: 32 random bytes base64url; in one admin transaction revoke all active records for the project, `set` `client_portal_tokens/{sha256}`, update project `clientPortal.enabled=true`, `activeTokenHash`, `tokenIssuedAt`, `updatedAt`.
   4. Response carries `url = portalUrl(token)`. `PortalLinkCard` keeps that URL even when the follow-up GET (triggered by the `enabled` flag change) returns `url: null` on a keyless deployment (`issuedAt` match merge, lines 65-75).
   5. Copy link / header Share / `s` / `c` → `handleShareClientLink` re-reads state and copies, or routes to the Client tab with a toast.

2. **Client opens the page**
   1. `page.tsx` → `loadPortal` (React `cache`) → `loadClientPortalByToken` → `verifyPortalToken`.
   2. Verify: malformed tokens hash to `sha256('')`; always reads the token doc and a project doc (sentinel id `client-portal-no-such-project-sentinel` when no record); requires well-formed, record exists, active, no `revokedAt`, unexpired, project exists, `clientPortal.enabled === true`, pointer equals hash (timing-safe). Any failure → `PortalAuthError(404)` → "This link is no longer active".
   3. Loader reads timeline, asks, checklists with firebase-admin and calls `buildClientPortalView`.
   4. `ClientPortalScreen` renders server-side; only `PortalReview` (gets `review`, `token`) and `TrackPortalView` (gets `token`) are serialized to the browser.

3. **View counting**
   1. `TrackPortalView`: skip if `preview`; once per browser session (`sessionStorage` key `portal-view-sent:<token>`); waits for Firebase `onAuthStateChanged` — signed-in user → no beacon; anonymous or 1.5 s timeout → `POST /api/portal/[token]/view` with `{}`.
   2. Route: verify token; skip `preview:true` and bot UAs; write `portal_views` row + merge `clientFacing` counters (no `updatedAt`) + `touchPortalToken` (token `lastUsedAt`, `useCount++`).
   3. Team sees "Opened N× · last …" in `PortalLinkCard` (from live project doc) and the list via `ViewsPopover` → `GET /views`.

4. **What the projection includes** (`buildClientPortalView`)
   1. The plan: `project.clientPlan` (normalised). With none saved, `legacyClientPlan` turns what an old portal showed into one: each timeline phase with a `clientVisible` milestone becomes a stage (those milestones its "what you get", their span its dates), unphased visible milestones an "Other" stage, visible manual links the project files, and the pin is `currentPhaseId` or the first phase with unfinished work (`PLAN_COMPLETE` when all are done).
   2. `resolveClientPlan` against the checklists: every section is classified into a stage kind (`classifySections`) and its items counted toward the stage of that kind (or the nearest earlier one the plan still has). Current stage: status Delivered → every stage done; `clientFacing.currentStageId` = `'__complete__'` → every stage done; a pinned stage id → that stage; otherwise the earliest stage with open steps, skipping a stage nothing tracks once a later stage has work ticked. Before it = done, after it = upcoming.
   3. Stages carry title, state, dates, deliverables and files; only the current stage carries `percent` (floored, so never 100 until every step is settled). Nothing else from the checklist leaves: no step titles, no section titles (except the review card's), no `kind`.
   4. Files: only http(s) URLs survive `normalizeClientPlan`. Website URL = `webflowConfig.customDomain`; a visible manual link titled live/production/website counts only on a pre-plan portal.
   5. `lastUpdateAt` = the latest of the team's `clientFacing.lastUpdateAt`, the plan's `updatedAt` and, when any stage is tracked, the newest checklist `updatedAt` (a tick moves the tracker, so it is news).
   6. Asks = tasks with `needsClientInput && status !== 'done'`, `{id,title,dueDate}` only, sorted by due date.
   7. Brand = `clientPortal.brandName` → `project.client` → `project.name`; logo = `brandLogoUrl` → `project.logoUrl`; agency contact = `reviewOwnerEmail` → `assigneeEmails[0]` (not `contactEmails`). Status via `normalizeClientStatus` + portal label.
   8. Review = first `client_review` section (checklists in load order, sections by `order`) without an approval, else the last one (with `approvedAt`, `approvedNote`). Section title only, never item titles.

5. **Client approves a stage**
   1. `PortalReview` → `POST /api/portal/[token]/approve { stageKey, note }` (button disabled when `preview`).
   2. Route verifies token, splits `stageKey`, loads `project_checklists/{checklistId}`, checks `projectId` match and `role === 'client_review'` (or legacy mapping), then in a transaction appends a `StageApproval` to `delivery.approvals` unless one exists for the key.
   3. No checklist item is ticked. Team sees it in the delivery module's `ClientApprovalCard`.

6. **Rotate / disable**
   1. `rotate` → `issuePortalToken` (revokes old in the same transaction). Old link fails verify immediately.
   2. `disable` → `revokePortalTokens(..., { disable: true })`: revoke all active, delete `activeTokenHash`, set `enabled=false`. Branding/status/visibility are kept; re-enabling issues a new token.

7. **Plan: drafted, edited, tracked**
   1. Drafted at creation by the New project dialog (project-links), only for a project made outside the four engagements: `draftClientPlan([AGENCY_START, ...template.sections, AGENCY_CLOSE], { startDate, endDate, templateId })`, i.e. the sections the checklist is about to get, written inside the same `createProject` call; the checklist is created right after.
   2. `planStageKinds`: the kinds the sections classify into, in canonical order (kickoff, discovery, design, build, review, launch, handover). The Webflow migration SOP gives all seven; the brand SOP gives Kickoff, Discovery, Design, Handover. Each stage gets `CLIENT_STAGE_DEFAULTS[kind]` (name, default days, starting "what you get" wording) and back-to-back dates from the start date, scaled to fit a target end date when one is given.
   3. The team edits in the Client tab; every change is one `updateClientPlan` transaction that normalises the plan and stamps `lastUpdateAt`. Rebuild re-drafts from the checklist, keeps the name, what-you-get lines and files of stages whose kind is still there, drops stages added by hand, and clears the pin.
   4. Live: ticking the checklist in the Delivery tab changes `resolveClientPlan`'s answer on the client's next page load. Since 2026-10-01 a plan is drafted only for a project made outside the four engagements, the Client tab shows it read-only, and nothing pins its stage.

## Gotchas and invariants

- **The Timeline wins over a saved plan.** `timelineStages(...).length > 0` decides it (projection, Client tab and Fathom filing all use it). A plan saved in the Client tab is kept but not shown while the Timeline has visible milestones; hiding every milestone hands the page back to the plan. `legacyClientPlan` now only applies to a portal with no plan *and* no timeline milestones (its link switches), and `currentPhaseId` is no longer honoured: the Now editor's pin is `currentStageId`, a phase id in Timeline mode.
- **Every milestone is visible unless hidden.** The old `clientVisible` flag is not read in Timeline mode (no project had set one). Internal-looking milestones need the eye toggle in the Client tab; milestone notes and assignees never leave the projection.
- **Nothing from Fathom is shown until shared.** The loader reads only `status == 'shared'` and the projection filters again. Attendees go out by name only (no name → left out, never the address), action-item owners likewise; the recording is `shareUrl`, never `fathomUrl`.
- **A re-sync never undoes the team.** It refreshes title, times, attendees, summary and action items, and the auto stage unless `phaseSetBy === 'team'`; `status` and `clientSummary` are never touched. An edited summary does not follow Fathom's later changes.
- **Matching is by email domain.** `meetingDomainsFor`: the team's list, else client-contact domains plus the custom domain. gmail.com-style providers, activeset.co and webflow.io/framer/vercel hosts never count. A call with two clients' domains is filed under both projects.
- **Summaries are parsed, not injected.** `parseSummary` builds blocks rendered with plain elements; a team edit is Markdown too. Links survive only through `safeHttpUrl`.
- **`<details>` groups are named** (`group/meeting`, `group/stage`): a meeting inside an opened stage otherwise rotates its chevron with the stage's.

- **A sheet is either read or written, never both.** A hand-kept sheet (bound) is only read; `bind` and `settings` write only `project_sheets`. An app-kept sheet (`managed`) is only written: `snapshotOf` returns null for it and `syncAllProjectSheets` skips it, so a typo in the sheet can never reach the portal, and the next write replaces it.
- **The service account cannot own files.** Its Drive quota is 0, so sheets are created in a Shared Drive (where the drive owns them) with `supportsAllDrives=true`; creating in My Drive or a normal shared folder fails. `drive.file` is not enough to create in a Shared Drive the app did not make, hence the full `drive` scope on those two calls.
- **The layout is applied once, at creation.** `layOut` sets widths, colours, rules and protections; a write only replaces values (`A1:…300`). Changing the look in code does not restyle sheets already made.
- **Tabs are recognised by name, never by position or content.** An unrecognised tab is never fetched, so copied-in data from another project (four real sheets carry LimeChat's URLs in `Sheet20`/`REDIRECTS`) cannot reach a portal. A recognised tab holding junk shows its first row as a sample in the Client tab; "Don't read this tab" drops it.
- **The sheet wins over the app's Timeline for stages** unless `stagesFrom: 'app'` and the app Timeline has stages. Sheet stage ids are `sheet-<phase cell>`, so a pinned stage survives syncs while the phase text stays; meetings land on sheet stages by date (their `phaseId` points at app phases).
- **The snapshot is replaced, not merged.** `syncProjectSheet` writes with `update()`; a set-merge would keep a deleted Timeline or a removed phase name alive inside the old map. A failed read keeps the previous snapshot and records `syncError`.
- **A read is written only if the binding still matches.** `writeIfStillBound` re-checks `spreadsheetId` inside a transaction, so an Unbind during a cron read cannot recreate the record and a re-bind mid-read cannot put the previous sheet's data on the portal (409 to the caller).
- **Only listed fields leave the sheet.** `FIELDS` has no notes, assignee, contact or money fields, Owner is reduced to client/team/both, and decisions are counted, not quoted. `project-sheet.portal.test.ts` checks the nested keys and that none of those values appear in the portal JSON.

- **The portal route must not import the module barrel.** `@/modules/client-portal` re-exports `'use client'` team UI (Client tab, repository); a route importing it ships ~326 KB of internal admin UI to the client. [page.tsx:6-9](../../src/app/portal/[token]/page.tsx) imports `ClientPortalScreen` directly; [eslint.config.mjs:84-95](../../eslint.config.mjs) turns off `no-restricted-imports` for `src/app/portal/**` with the explanation.
- **Server-only libs never go through the barrel.** `client-portal-tokens.ts`, `client-portal-auth.ts`, `client-portal.ts` start with `import 'server-only'`.
- **Adding a field to `ClientPortalView` is a product decision.** Keep `CLIENT_PORTAL_VIEW_KEYS` in step; the test asserts exact keys (on the view and on each stage) and that sentinel values on internal fields never appear.
- **The page is only as live as the checklist.** For steps (sheet, checklist, Timeline), `resolveTimelinePlan` puts the project in the furthest stage where a step that is not the client's has started while it has anything open, else the next stage with something to do, so a forgotten tick in Kickoff does not hold the client there; the earlier stage reads "3/4 done" rather than "Done". A plan (no steps) still picks the earliest stage with open checklist items.
- **Stage kinds come from section titles.** `kindOfSection` reads titles first (most specific patterns first: "pre-launch" is review, "post-launch" is handover), then the role. A custom SOP with unusual titles may group oddly; unmatched sections join the stage before them and the walk never goes backwards. Change the patterns with the real built-in SOPs' test cases in `client-plan.test.ts` green.
- **No pins.** `clientFacing.currentStageId` is no longer read for a saved plan or any step source (none was set when the Now card went). An old portal's `currentPhaseId` still places its legacy plan.
- **Delivered wins.** A client status of Delivered shows every stage done, whatever the checklist says. The status itself is set from the project card.
- **Files are http(s) only**, checked twice: the editor refuses anything else, and `normalizeClientPlan` drops it again on every write and before every render. A bare `figma.com/...` is stored as `https://`.
- **Pre-plan portals keep working.** Until a plan is saved, the projection rebuilds the old page from `clientVisible` milestones and links (`legacyClientPlan`). No UI sets those flags any more: the Timeline tab's "Visible to client" switch and the Client tab's switch lists are gone. The Client tab offers "Keep these stages" (saves the same thing as a real plan and pins where it was) or a fresh plan from the checklist.
- **Plan edits count as telling the client something**: `updateClientPlan` stamps `clientFacing.lastUpdateAt`, which clears the stale-portal nudge.
- **One live link, by transaction + pointer.** Issue/revoke run in `adminDb.runTransaction` ([client-portal-tokens.ts:153-165, 182-194](../../src/lib/client-portal-tokens.ts)); verify requires the project's `activeTokenHash` to equal the hash ([:279-295](../../src/lib/client-portal-tokens.ts)). A stray active record can never resolve.
- **Uniform rejection.** Every failure path does the same two reads and throws the same 404 text. The no-record sentinel id must be a *valid* Firestore id: `__none__` is reserved and caused a 500 on every portal request ([client-portal-tokens.ts:240-252](../../src/lib/client-portal-tokens.ts)).
- **`enable` never revives a previously shared link** when the portal was switched off out-of-band ([link/route.ts:97-105](../../src/app/api/client-portal/[projectId]/link/route.ts)).
- **The raw token is never stored in the clear.** Doc id is SHA-256; optional ciphertext needs `CLIENT_PORTAL_TOKEN_KEY`. Changing the key makes existing links unreadable in the UI but does not invalidate them — rotate links for that.
- **The page loader is read-only; only the beacon counts.** Slack unfurls and previews never count. The comment at [page.tsx:23-24](../../src/app/portal/[token]/page.tsx) saying the loader "bumps useCount" is stale.
- **Client-driven writes never touch `updatedAt`** (beacon merge [view/route.ts:81-87](../../src/app/api/portal/[token]/view/route.ts); approval [approve/route.ts:105-107](../../src/app/api/portal/[token]/approve/route.ts)), so client activity cannot reorder the team's `updatedAt`-sorted lists. Team writes do bump it.
- **Freshness comes from the checklist.** `checklistService.updateItemStatus` stamps `clientFacing.lastUpdateAt`/`lastUpdateBy` on the project after every tick (best effort, never `updatedAt`, so lists do not reshuffle). `lastUpdateAt` is the sole input to the stale nudge; the project card's status change deliberately omits `touch` ([ProjectCard.tsx:143-152](../../src/components/projects/ProjectCard.tsx)). `updateClientFacing`, `markClientUpdated` and `updateClientPlan` still stamp it but have no caller on the Client tab any more.
- **Plan writes are transactions** (`updateClientPlan` in [database.ts](../../src/services/database.ts)): the repository passes a pure edit that runs against the latest stored plan, so two people editing different stages at once do not undo each other. Firestore may run it more than once; keep edits free of side effects.
- **Asks have no switch.** Any not-done task with `needsClientInput` publishes its title verbatim. The flag is never cleared on completion, so the loader reads up to 500 flagged tasks and filters status in the projection ([client-portal.ts:88-92](../../src/lib/client-portal.ts)).
- **"Open site" is never guessed from an invisible link** — only the custom domain, or, on a pre-plan portal, a switched-on manual link ([client-portal.projection.ts](../../src/modules/client-portal/domain/client-portal.projection.ts) `detectWebsiteUrl`).
- **Approval is not a lock and ticks nothing.** Written only from `delivery.approvals`; the stage must be a real `client_review` section of a checklist belonging to the token's project ([approve/route.ts:66-83](../../src/app/api/portal/[token]/approve/route.ts)). `roleOf` is duplicated in the route and projection — keep them in step with delivery's.
- **Preview only disables Approve in the UI.** The approve route has no preview concept; a team member opening the real link (without `?preview=1`) can press Approve and it will record as the client's.
- **Signed-in team members are not counted**, but only if Firebase auth restores within 1.5 s; the settle logic in [TrackPortalView.tsx:52-73](../../src/modules/client-portal/ui/components/TrackPortalView.tsx) prevents the fallback timer double-firing.
- **Day math is viewer-local.** `daysSinceClientUpdate` converts the stored instant to the viewer's calendar day before differencing ([client-status.ts:19-22](../../src/modules/client-portal/domain/client-status.ts)).
- **Vercel preview deployments sit behind Vercel SSO**, so a portal link on a preview cannot be opened anonymously; test on production (`app.activeset.co` serves public routes). `CLIENT_PORTAL_TOKEN_KEY` must be identical across Production/Preview/Development because they share one Firebase project.
